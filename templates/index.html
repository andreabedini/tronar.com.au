At home on sandy ground

We select our cows so they hold their condition on our light, sandy Western Australian soil. That makes them hardy enough for very different country. Tronar bulls now work from the Gascoyne and the Pilbara to the South Coast, and Tronar bloodlines have gone to Queensland, New South Wales, Victoria and China.
