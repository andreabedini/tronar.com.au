We have limited stock of the impressive Tronar Cameron's semen. We are exceptionally proud of Tronar Cameron. He is, without a doubt, the best Red Angus bull we have produced since we started in 1976. He has a strong phenotype, producing offspring with his medium birth weight, exceptional growth, above average frame score, exceptional temperament (he is a lovely placid bull and his offspring are the same), well above average milk production, straight backs and free moving, strong feet and in the top 1% of intramuscular fat. We are keeping most of the heifers he produces for our herd. His daughter G35 went to the [Red Angus National Sale and Show](http://www.centralwesterndaily.com.au/story/126561/bidding-reaches-14000-at-red-angus-sale/) in Dubbo and won her division and went on to sell to Kate and Richard Shaw of QLD for \$5500.

We also have some commercial semen available including Tronar Bovine B112 who has a good stretch of estimated breeding values (EBVs). His dam is Tronar Westwood W11 and sire is Leachman Vaquero. There are a handful of Tronar Zorro straws left. Zorro has been heavily used in the Tronar herd for his excellent EBVs. He's also a double cross of our herd matriarch Tronar Q14—a cow who we are very proud of who has an excellent temperament, a wonderfully deep body and dam of 32 registered progeny. Zorro has consistently packed performance into his progeny which is no surprise when you look at his pedigree, his grandfathers are international high-performance sires.
