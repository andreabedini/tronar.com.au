Two champion steers at the 2024 Perth Royal Show were sired by Tronar Red Head (3LWR125). Both were bred and prepared by Peter and Rebecca Bantock of Red Rock Red Angus, Boyanup.

The Bantocks' 530 kg Red Angus–Simmental steer won Grand Champion Extra Heavy Weight Carcass. It scored 95.48 points on the carcase, and also won Highest Scoring Owner Bred Animal.

A second Red Rock steer, a Red Angus–Simmental exhibited by Ella Clarke, won Grand Champion Extra Heavy Weight Steer on the hoof and Reserve Champion Steer of the show. Peter and Rebecca gave Ella the steer to thank her for her help with their show team, and let her choose it from the herd.

Congratulations to Peter, Rebecca and Ella, and thank you for showing what a Tronar sire can do.

The results were published in the Red Angus Society of Australia's 2025 Annual and in Farm Weekly.
