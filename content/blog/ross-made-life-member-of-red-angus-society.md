At its 2021 annual general meeting, the Red Angus Society of Australia made Ross a Life Member. Ross retired from the Society's Council that year after more than twenty years as a councillor. In that time he was the first Western Australian to serve as Federal President, and he also served as Federal Treasurer.

Fellow West Australian breeder Peter Cowcher gave the commendation at the meeting. He described Ross as "the mainstay of Red Angus in our state", and spoke about how generous Ross has been with advice for young and new breeders:

> “I was certainly a beneficiary of this when I was starting out as a stud breeder in Red Angus.”
>
> Peter Cowcher

The Collard family joined the Red Angus Society soon after it was formed in the 1970s, and the Tronar stud has been breeding Red Angus since 1976.
