The Red Angus Society of Australia publishes which sires the calves registered each year come from. The latest figures show how much work Tronar bulls are doing in other breeders' herds.

For calves born from 2021 to 2023, Tronar Pacific Tide was the most used natural-mating Red Angus sire in Australia, with 132 registered calves. Five of the top 15 natural-mating sires were Tronar bulls:

- Tronar Pacific Tide (3LWP013): 132 calves
- Tronar Neuorpur (3LWN093): 53 calves
- Tronar Popeye Pete (3LWP146): 52 calves
- Tronar Myalup (3LWM175): 46 calves
- Tronar Quid (3LWQ031): 36 calves

A year earlier, for calves born from 2020 to 2022, Tronar Neuorpur was equal second on the Society's list of top Red Angus sires, with 71 registered calves.

Three Tronar bulls are also in the top 50 Red Angus sires listed in the Society's 2025 Annual: Tronar Pacific Tide, Tronar Cameron and Tronar Neuorpur.

These figures come from the Society's 2023, 2024 and 2025 Annuals. To talk about Tronar bulls, call Ross and Tracy.
