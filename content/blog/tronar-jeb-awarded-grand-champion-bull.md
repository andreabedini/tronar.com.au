Tronar Jeb took home the Grand Champion Red Angus Bull gong at the 2014 IGA Perth Royal Show. Earlier in the year Jeb won Interbreed Champion at the Harvey Show. For those with a keen eye you might recognise Jeb as the little bull icon on our website. Jeb went on to [top the 2015 Bandeeka sale](http://www.redangus.org.au/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=264:jeb-tops-bandeeka-sale&catid=42:admin&Itemid=163), selling for \$8000. Jeb has a beautiful, quiet temperament and follows in his grandmother Q14's footsteps. He was a standout bull calf from day one. He was very eye-catching, standing perfectly even when he was a calf.

## Other Tronar wins at the 2014 IGA Perth Royal Show

Tronar Jamison<em> 3LWJ118 </em>won best Red Angus bull born between 01/04/2013 and 30/06/2013.

Tronar Jewel <em>3LWJ024 </em>came third for a Red Angus female born between 01/01/2013 and 31/03/2013.
