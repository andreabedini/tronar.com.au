Tronar progeny have had a successful run at this year's IGA Perth Royal Show. The impressive [Bandeeka Red Lord](http://www.pressreader.com/australia/countryman/20160929/281655369562584), son of [Tronar Jeb](/tronar-jeb-awarded-grand-champion-bull/) and Tronar Hilary, took the All Breeds Supreme Junior Champion Bull crown after winning the Red Angus Supreme Champion Bull. Tronar Jeb has shown his award-winning genetics pass to his progeny.

Other Tronar progeny who took home awards were:

- Bandeeka Lollie, daughter of Tronar Gash, won Reserve Junior Champion Red Angus Female.
- Bandeeka Red Lustre, son of Tronar Jeb, came second in the Red Angus Bull Born Between 01/01/2015 and 31/03/2015.
- Bandeeka won the Red Angus Group of Three prize with two bulls sired by Tronar Jeb.

We congratulate the Kitchens at Bandeeka for another successful year at the show!
